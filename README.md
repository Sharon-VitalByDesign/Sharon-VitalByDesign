# Dr. Sharon Matthews

I'm an educator, learning designer, AI consultant, and founder of [Vital by Design](https://vitalbydesign.io).

For more than 25 years, I've worked at the intersection of teaching, learning, and innovation. Much of that work has involved challenging assumptions about how people learn, helping educators rethink their practice, and designing learning experiences that make thinking, problem-solving, and meaningful application central to the process.

More recently, I've been exploring what happens when people with ideas—but without traditional software development backgrounds—have the tools to bring those ideas to life.

That's what brought me to AI-assisted development and tools like OpenAI Codex.

I'm not a software engineer. I approach building from the perspective of a learning designer: identifying a problem worth solving, imagining what a better experience might look like, and figuring out how to make it work. Sometimes that involves a lot of experimentation, a few spectacular failures, and an unreasonable number of revisions.

But that's part of the fun.

## What I'm Building

### [Gamemaker's Toolkit: Arena Builder](https://github.com/Sharon-VitalByDesign/Official-Gamemaker-Toolkit)

An AI-powered lesson design tool that helps educators move beyond traditional instructional approaches to create learning experiences that foster critical thinking, student agency, productive struggle, and meaningful application.

Built around my own pedagogical framework, the Toolkit uses AI to support educators as designers of learning experiences rather than simply generators of instructional materials.

### [Lift Lab](https://github.com/Sharon-VitalByDesign/LiftLab)

An exploration of how AI can support learning without doing the thinking for the learner.

Lift Lab focuses on scaffolding that adapts to what learners need in the moment, helping them develop independence, confidence, and ownership of their learning rather than becoming dependent on AI-generated answers.

### [Mammal Crime Unit: Alpha-Gal Tracker](https://github.com/Sharon-VitalByDesign/alpha-gal-food-symptom-tracker-mammal-crime-unit)

A personal project born out of a very real problem: tracking food exposures, symptoms, and patterns while navigating the complexities of a suspected mammalian-product allergy.

A good example of what becomes possible when you can build a tool around a specific need instead of trying to make an existing product fit.

## How I Think About Technology

I'm interested in technology for what it makes possible, not simply what it can do.

In education, that means asking whether a tool actually changes the learning experience, strengthens human thinking, or creates opportunities that didn't previously exist.

In development, it means starting with a problem, bringing domain expertise and a clear vision to the process, and using AI as a collaborator to turn that vision into something tangible.

I believe some of the most interesting innovations will come from people who understand a problem deeply but have never considered themselves technology creators.

I'm enjoying being one of them.

**AI isn't the innovation. Intentional design is.**
